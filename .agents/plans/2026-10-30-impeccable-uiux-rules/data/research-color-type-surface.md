Points where sources disagree:
- C3: NN/g and Malewicz endorse 60-30-10 for UI. An interior designer (Jasmine Alley) argues against literal use.
- C7: Atlassian pairs border with shadow on flat cards. Refactoring UI, as summarized by updivision.com, prefers shadow over borders.
- C14: Icon sizing anchor differs. Humbl uses cap height; damato uses line-height.
- C17: NN/g says more blur helps on busy backgrounds. Axess warns against blur of 20px or more.
- C19: Designary's formula uses outer thickness/2. Every other source uses padding.

Weak sources (blogs, vendor pages, low-authority) are used only where no primary source exists: C4 blue=trust, C8 max-sizes wording, C11 2-3 fonts, C15, C7 shadow tinting.
